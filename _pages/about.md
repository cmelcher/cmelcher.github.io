# Cody Melcher  
PhD Candidate in Statistics and Optimization  
University of Arizona  

Welcome! I am a PhD student in the **Systems and Industrial Engineering** department at the University of Arizona.  
My research focuses on **optimization theory and algorithms**, with particular interest in **saddle point problems, semi-infinite programming, and distributionally robust optimization (DRO)**. I develop first-order methods with theoretical guarantees, and apply them to problems in robust multi-task learning, TinyML, and reinforcement learning.  

## Research Interests
- Convex and nonconvex optimization  
- Min-max algorithms and saddle points  
- Semi-infinite constrained optimization  
- Robust and distributionally robust learning  
- Applications to TinyML and reinforcement learning
